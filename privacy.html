Wakery Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Wakery (“we”, “us”) is a mission-based alarm app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. Wakery is designed so you can use core alarm features without creating an account.

1. Information we process

  • Alarm and wake data (on device / optional iCloud sync). Alarms you create, wake-up history, and related settings are stored on your device. If you sign in with Apple and turn on iCloud sync, this data may sync through your personal iCloud account using Apple’s Key-Value Storage. We do not operate our own account server.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose to sign in, Apple may provide a user identifier and, if you share them, a name and email. We use this only to recognize your account on the device and support sync / account deletion.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive purchase status (for example, whether Wakery Pro is active) through StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising (non‑Pro users). If you are not subscribed to Wakery Pro, we show ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. You may see an App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, ads can still appear in a less personalized form.
  • Diagnostics you send. If you email support, your message may include device model, iOS version, app version, and language that our app pre-fills to help troubleshooting.

2. How we use information

  • To run alarms, missions, reports, and settings
  • To sync data across your devices when you enable iCloud sync
  • To unlock Wakery Pro and restore purchases
  • To show ads to free users and measure ad performance (via Google)
  • To respond to support requests

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data is processed by:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, iCloud, App Store, notifications, and (where available) AlarmKit
  • Google AdMob — advertising for non‑Pro users. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Tracking

On iOS, “tracking” follows Apple’s definition. Wakery may request permission via App Tracking Transparency for personalized ads. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Local alarm data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • If iCloud sync is on, removing cloud data is available from Account settings (Delete Account clears synced cloud records associated with the app’s key-value store; local alarms on the device may remain unless you clear them).
  • Signing out stops associating the Apple identity on that device.

6. Children

Wakery is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. International users

Depending on your region, Google’s User Messaging Platform may show a consent form before personalized ads. You can revisit ad privacy choices in the app when required by law (for example, “Ad Privacy Options”).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states, or the European Economic Area and United Kingdom), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Most of your data lives on your device or in your own iCloud account, so you can delete it directly in the App or in iOS Settings. For any request that you cannot complete yourself, contact us using the email below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Wakery after an update means you accept the revised policy.
